About

Engineering judgement behind every simulation

I’m a computational engineer with extensive industrial experience in CFD simulation, thermal-fluid analysis and predictive simulation. My work covers the full workflow: geometry preparation and meshing, numerical model definition, validation against experimental data, and technical reporting.

At BMW Group I was responsible for design evaluation and approval based on predictive simulation results, balancing performance, efficiency and safety. I also build the tooling around the simulations: scripting, parallel computing and process optimisation that make the work faster and reproducible.

I present findings through clear visualisations and concise reports, so that multidisciplinary teams can make decisions without needing to read the solver log.
